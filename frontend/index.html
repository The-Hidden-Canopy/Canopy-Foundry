What belongs where

STANDALONE FIRST

Foundry is the local execution product. Authority, identity, organization scope, lifecycle, evaluation, and promotion remain outside it.

FOUNDRY

Foundry owns

  • Request admission and plan resolution
  • Memory, precision, kernels, routing, and optimizer execution
  • Checkpoint integrity and local execution receipts
  • Deterministic rejection of unsupported plans
EXTERNAL

The Hub owns

  • Identity, organization scope, roles, and authentication
  • Job lifecycle, quotas, cancellation, and worker claims
  • Evaluation, audit, promotion, and public projections
  • Remote API authority and policy selection
